# Desert Victory Described

A spoken audio-description companion for the 1943 British official film *Desert Victory*. Play the film in another tab, and this page speaks short descriptions in the gaps in the commentary, with optional historian’s notes.

Live: https://ahsantheman55-blip.github.io/desert-victory-described/

Timed to the public-domain Internet Archive copy [DesertVictory](https://archive.org/details/DesertVictory), file `DesertVictory.mp4`, running **1 hour 10 minutes 29.5 seconds**. That file includes the film itself (ending at about 1 hour 1 minute) and then a short American official newsreel, Film Bulletin number 47. Do not use a shorter copy, or the timings will drift.

The original film is a work of the British Crown whose copyright has expired. This companion’s descriptions and notes were written for this app.

Not tested on an iPhone or with VoiceOver.
